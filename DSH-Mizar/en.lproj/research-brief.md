# Note-taking workflow brief

This comparison uses two sample workflows for a small interview study.

## Rapid capture

Record one line per insight during the interview. After each meeting, group the notes into themes. This keeps the interview moving, but checking a quote later can take longer.

## Traceable capture

Record each observation alongside its source ID and timestamp. This adds a step during the interview and makes it easier for a teammate to review the evidence later.

## Pilot

Try both workflows on two practice interviews. Compare the time spent taking notes and the time a teammate needs to find the source of three observations.
